# What the notation showcase covers

The library’s notation showcase is a generated piano score with 36 written bars, numbered 0–35. It follows the same MusicXML import path as a score opened from a file. The audition plays the written bars in performance order, with the repeat and jump taken near the end. The source is [scripts/showcase.ts](../scripts/showcase.ts) and the bundled score is [public/demos/showcase.musicxml](../public/demos/showcase.musicxml).

| Written bars | What to look and listen for |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4 | One-beat pickup, whole through thirty-second notes, single dots, ledger lines on both sides, mixed beams with hooks and up to three levels, crescendo hairpin, and explicit sharp, flat, natural and double accidentals. |
| 5–9 | Eighth and quarter triplets, a sixteenth quintuplet, visible and hidden tuplet numbers, a bracket, diminuendo hairpin, two voices on one staff, displaced notes a second apart, rests from the full-bar rest through the thirty-second, and ties within and across bars, including both notes of a chord. |
| 10–13 | 4/4 to 6/8 and back, three flats followed by two sharps, bass-to-treble-to-bass clef on the lower staff, dotted-quarter and quarter metronome marks, p and f, una corda and tre corde, 8va and 8vb, and a sounding range from A0 to A6. |
| 14–19 | Six articulations, including staccato plus accent on one note, slurs on both staves with the bass slur crossing a barline, a held fermata, sustain written with signs or a notched line, selective sostenuto, and shifted noteheads in seconds and clusters. |
| 20–22 | Slashed and unslashed graces, a beamed grace group, a trill with a wavy line, both mordents, a turn and a delayed turn with a lower natural, single-note and two-note tremolos, and an upward arpeggio shared by both staves. |
| 23–27 | Headless eighths sharing melody notes, a hidden rest, octave-transposing clefs, cue-size sounding notes, fingering and substitution, a parenthesized sounding note, and ritardando. |
| 28–32 | A tempo, repeat signs and two voltas, segno, D.S. al Coda, To Coda and coda. The playback order is 28, 29, 28, 30, 31, 28, 30, 32. Bar 32 demonstrates upright and inverted fermatas and sustain written with both a sign and a line. |
| 33–35 | Four eighths on one beam crossing from bass to treble, a return to 6/8 with a double-dotted eighth rest, then 7/8 grouped 2+2+3 over a double-dotted bass half note. |

## Limits visible in the import and engraving code

- MusicXML import and staff engraving retain both dots, as in Comptine d’un autre été, bars 91, 95 and 99. The [duration model](../src/domain/notation/noteValue.ts) still ends at the thirty-second note. A 64th note keeps its imported playback length and uses a thirty-second symbol.
- Showcase bar 35 contains a `light-heavy` final barline in its source. The [barline reader](../src/infrastructure/parsers/musicxml/PartReader.ts) stores repeat and ending data and discards explicit bar styles. The same limit applies to standalone double barlines such as the one in Czardas bar 13. Signature-change and repeat barlines are generated by their own rendering rules.
- The showcase uses one uncompressed, single-part MusicXML piano score. Format coverage for compressed MusicXML, MIDI and PDF belongs to their respective import fixtures.

## Printed fixture for PDF import

The [two-page A4 score](../tests/fixtures/pdf/showcase.pdf) is engraved with Verovio independently
of Claviano. It embeds Bravura glyphs, so the PDF reader can inspect the printed music symbols.
Run `npm run samples:pdf` to compare both pages with the bundled MusicXML, even in a checkout
without private samples. The report distinguishes staff pairs and located markings from a
successfully reconstructed score. For bars 33–35 it also compares beamed stem groups, the
printed metre on both staves, and the paired augmentation dots with the corresponding MusicXML.

The MusicXML preview includes all 36 written bars, with 263 principal notes and five grace notes. It starts with the
one-beat pickup and continues through mixed beams, a crescendo and diminuendo, explicit
accidentals, triplets and a quintuplet, chords, overlapping voices, and rests through
thirty-second notes. Bars 8–11 add ties between repeated notes and across a barline in a chord,
a change to three flats and 6/8, a dotted-quarter metronome mark, and a treble clef for the lower staff.
Bars 12–14 restore the bass clef, change to two sharps and 4/4, and include p and f,
una corda and tre corde, sustain pedal signs, 8va and 8vb, and articulations on both staves.
Bars 15–17 add a fermata, slurs within and across systems, a signed sustain pedal, and
a pedal line with a change between two harmonies. Bar 18 has an unsigned bracket below the
staff and the heading “Sostenuto” above it. The heading can also describe how to play the notes,
so the reader records the span in the report and keeps the notes without assigning a pedal type.
The second page begins with displaced seconds and dotted chords in bar 19. In bar 20,
five grace notes in four groups retain their printed rhythms, slashes, accidental and slur.
Bar 21 adds a trill with a wavy line, both mordents and two turns, including a delayed turn
with a lower natural. Bar 22 adds single-note and two-note tremolos and an ascending arpeggio
across both staves. Bars 23–26 preserve shared heads, octave clefs, the smaller sounding voice,
fingerings and the parenthesized note. The PDF places all eight fingerings above the notes;
the MusicXML source leaves the side unspecified. The silent beginning of the bass part in bar 23 is
timed against the upper voice. Bars 27–32 preserve the tempo instructions, repeats, both
endings and the coda jump. The second ending is printed as `2`, while the MusicXML source
labels it `2.`. The final three bars preserve the beam crossing the staves, double-dotted
durations and the changes to 6/8 and 7/8. The exported score plays 39 bars including repeats.

The reader checks the printed number when it moves to a new system and
checks rhythmic durations and shared onsets before appending a bar. The report lists
playback-only tempo and dynamics from the source MusicXML separately from the printed notation.

The printed export shows pedal brackets in bars 17, 18 and 32. Verovio uses a vertical tick
for the pedal change in bar 17 and omits the requested `Ped.` sign alongside the final bracket
in bar 32. Use the PDF as the reference for what is actually visible in these bars.

After changing the MusicXML, regenerate the PDF with `python scripts/showcase-pdf.py`. The
[export script](../scripts/showcase-pdf.py) lists its dependencies. A test checks the MusicXML
digest stored in the PDF so that the two cannot silently get out of sync.

## Verify the showcase

Run `npm run showcase` after editing the generator. This rewrites the bundled MusicXML. The command below runs the focused suites and snapshots.

```sh
npm test -- --run tests/showcase.test.ts tests/showcasePlaying.test.ts tests/showcaseChanges.test.ts tests/snapshot.test.ts
```
